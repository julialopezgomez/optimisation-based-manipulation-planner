Reachability A*: run time as the number of caps grows

One scene: the Panda arm, 8 bottle caps on a 0.5 m arc around it and 3 walls between groups of caps (multicap_scene.py); C-free is 356 convex sets in the 9-D arm + finger space (cfree_9dof_8caps_3obs_capsafe.yaml). For each K, 20 random queries: K random caps turn by random amounts in [0.5, π] rad summing to 2.5·K rad, between random start and goal configurations. The A* may grasp only those K caps; every cap stays in its state. Each query is solved by the weighted A* (the notebook's default, w = 1.5: guaranteed at most 1.5× the fewest actions) . Measured on this machine (AMD Ryzen 9 9950X 16-Core Processor, 32 threads, 63 GB).
