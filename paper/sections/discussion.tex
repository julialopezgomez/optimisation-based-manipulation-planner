\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{Certify where it matters}
The weakest link is not the search but the sets it searches over
(Section~\ref{sec:results:fidelity}). Grasp pieces are thin and almost
one-dimensional, so they are much cheaper to validate than full regions. They
can be swept densely with a collision checker, split into validated wrist
intervals, or certified with C-IRIS~\cite{dai2024certified}, and regions can be
seeded on grasp sets so that the decomposition is dense where regrasps happen.
The search consumes only pieces and their wrist ranges, so it does not change.

\paragraph{Scope}
Exact intervals rely on Assumption~\ref{ass:task}, which covers caps, knobs,
valves and cranks but not general pick-and-place. With more object
coordinates, a node's reachable object configurations become sets that could
be propagated with Minkowski sums and intersections as in
CASSR~\cite{wang2026cassr}, at the cost of exact dominance and with nonlinear
grasp transforms to approximate.

\paragraph{Optimality}
The search minimises grasp actions, and then set changes, a weak proxy for
length. As in CASSR, the sets are fixed before configurations are placed, so
path length is not optimal. A convex refinement over the chosen sets would
make each path locally optimal for its sequence, not globally. The heuristic
uses the longest piece in the map. A heuristic computed per connected
component of the graph would be tighter.

\paragraph{Grasp polytopes and offline cost}
Our grasp polytope comes from one seed and a linearisation, and is valid near
the seed only. More seeds from NLP sampling would cover other components of
the grasp manifold. Merging overlapping pieces would allow longer strokes, but
then placement must follow a chain of pieces. Growing IRIS regions from grasp
samples produced many tiny polytopes, limited by the curvature of the
manifold. A new obstacle layout requires a new decomposition, the dominant
offline cost: in a separate run, IRIS-NP2 took 120\,s for 25 regions at a
0.98 coverage target \tocheck{different settings}. GPU
implementations~\cite{werner2025superfast} reduce this cost, and new start and
goal queries reuse the graph unchanged.
