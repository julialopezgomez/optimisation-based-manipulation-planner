\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We posed multi-regrasp manipulation planning as a reachability problem over a
convex decomposition of free space, and solved it with an A* search whose
nodes pair polytopes with intervals of reachable object configurations. This
transfers to manipulation an idea that recently matured in footstep planning.
For objects with one coordinate coupled to the wrist, propagation is interval
arithmetic and dominance is exact. The graph needs only LPs, and the search
returns the minimum number of grasp actions relative to the decomposition,
without fixing the number of regrasps in advance and without the numerical
fragility of a mixed-integer formulation. On a 10-DOF Franka cap-turning task
it plans in milliseconds where the MIQP fails. The same experiment shows that
these guarantees are only as good as the decomposition near contact. We
believe that certifying the switch sets, rather than covering free space, is
the key requirement for planners of this kind.
