\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Manipulation and regrasp planning}
Regrasp planners first searched over finite sets of grasps and
placements~\cite{tournassoud1987regrasping,alami1990geometrical}, an approach
that continues in regrasp graphs over thousands of precomputed
grasps~\cite{wan2017regrasp}. Lertkultanon and
Pham~\cite{lertkultanon2018certified} certify completeness for bimanual
regrasping relative to such a discretisation. The manipulation
graph~\cite{simeon2004manipulation} made grasps and placements continuous, and
was generalised to multi-modal
planning~\cite{hauser2010multimodal,hauser2011randomized}, asymptotically
optimal sampling~\cite{schmitt2017optimal,vegabrown2016asymptotically} and
constraint graphs with constrained
samplers~\cite{mirabel2016hpp,berenson2011tsr,kingston2018sampling}. TAMP adds
symbolic decisions on top~\cite{garrett2021integrated,garrett2020pddlstream}.
In optimisation-based TAMP, an outer search enumerates skeletons with a fixed
number of mode switches~\cite{toussaint2015lgp}, and regrasp
maps~\cite{levit2025regrasp} guide that search towards regions where several
regrasps are possible. Closest in spirit to our work, Liu
et al.~\cite{liu2026graph} precompute a graph of mutually reachable sets of
object orientations and grasps for planar contact-rich manipulation. We also
reason about reachable sets. Ours are polytopes of the robot--object
configuration space, and we guarantee completeness and the minimum number of
grasps relative to the decomposition.

\subsection{Reachability in contact planning}
Our search follows CASSR~\cite{wang2026cassr}. There, a node is the subset of a
contact surface reachable from its parent. It is expanded by Minkowski sums
and intersections with neighbouring surfaces, and a quadratic program places
the footsteps once the surfaces are fixed. Regrasping changes two things.
Reachability grows only inside grasp pieces, and with one coupled object
coordinate the reachable sets are intervals. Dominance is therefore exact and
the heuristic admissible, whereas CASSR scales its heuristic, which then is
not admissible, and discretises yaw.

\subsection{Convex decompositions and Graphs of Convex Sets}
Configuration-space obstacles cannot be described exactly in
general~\cite{lozano1983spatial,canny1988complexity}, so free space is
inner-approximated by convex sets. IRIS grows such sets by alternating
separating hyperplanes and inscribed ellipsoids~\cite{deits2015computing}.
C-IRIS certifies them~\cite{amice2022finding,dai2024certified}, while IRIS-NP,
IRIS-NP2 and IRIS-ZO trade certification for speed and give probabilistic
guarantees~\cite{petersen2023growing,werner2024faster}. Clique covers automate
the seeding~\cite{werner2024approximating}, and GPU implementations approach
online rates~\cite{werner2025superfast}. Graphs of Convex Sets (GCS) find
optimal paths over decompositions through a tight convex
relaxation~\cite{marcucci2024shortest,marcucci2023motion}. Extensions cover
non-Euclidean joints~\cite{cohn2023noneuclidean}, temporal
logic~\cite{kurtz2023temporal} and planar contact-rich
manipulation~\cite{graesdal2024tight}, and heuristic searches handle graphs too
large to solve at once~\cite{chia2024gcsstar,natarajan2024implicit}. GCS*
prunes partial paths by domination checks on reachable sets, which in general
must be approximated~\cite{chia2024gcsstar}. With our reachable sets, the
check is exact.
