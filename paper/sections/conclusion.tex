\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We represented the manipulation graph of multi-regrasp tasks with convex
sets, so that the switches between moving alone and moving the object are
convex transition sets, and searched them with an A* whose nodes pair a set
with the interval of object configurations reachable inside it. For objects with one coordinate
coupled to the wrist, the search is complete and returns the fewest grasps the
sets allow, without fixing that number in advance and without integer
variables. The search thus carries over to regrasping an idea matured in footstep
planning, that of searching over convex reachable sets rather than individual
contacts, and extends it to contact sets that have to be constructed and to
contacts that move the object. Its guarantees are only as good as the
transition sets are faithful near contact, which is why we validate them. \todo{one sentence on E1--E5 once run.}
