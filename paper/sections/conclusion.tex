\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We presented a manipulation planner that operates on convex decompositions of
the collision-free composite configuration space of robot and object. By
representing the grasp and placement spaces as unions of polytopes and
intersecting them with the decomposition, the nodes and edges of the classical
manipulation graph become convex sets. Grasp and release configurations are
then selected with the minimum number of grasps by mixed-integer optimisation
or heuristic search, and connected by transit and transfer trajectories
computed with Graphs of Convex Sets. To scale this representation to a 7-DOF
arm, where grasping is a nonlinear constraint on the forward kinematics, we
combined NLP sampling for diverse grasp configurations with polytopes aligned
with the null space of the grasp constraints. \todo{one or two sentences on
the 10-DOF results and the comparison with sampling-based planners.} More
broadly, this work shows that describing where grasping actions can start and
end as convex sets lets an optimiser reason about how to sequence them.
